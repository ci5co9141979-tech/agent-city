QUOTESMITH

Double-click quotesmith.html. It runs offline in any modern browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Rate calculator tab: find your minimum hourly rate, click 'Use this as my default rate'.
2. Build tab: describe the project, click 'Suggest line items', adjust hours, add client details.
3. Print / Save PDF gives the clean client-facing quote. Save keeps it in this browser.

Note: suggestions are rule-based hour templates, not AI. Quotes are saved in your browser's local storage on this device.
